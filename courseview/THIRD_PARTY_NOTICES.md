## Provenance hashes

sourceImageSha256 is carried from source review metadata and can identify pre-encoding input. imageSha256 / overlay.assetSha256 identify the shipped encoded overview bytes. data/asset-manifest.json gives every shipped/optional image's actual bytes and SHA256. Never substitute a provenance hash for the distribution checksum.
